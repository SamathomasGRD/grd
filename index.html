Questions & Answers

Everything You Need to Know

What are your hours of operation? ▼
We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on major federal holidays. We do offer limited care during school breaks — please contact us for availability.
What is your teacher-to-child ratio? ▼
We maintain industry-leading ratios: Infants 1:4, Young Toddlers 1:5, Toddlers 1:6, Preschool 1:8, and School-Age 1:12. These ratios allow for individualized attention and ensure every child feels seen and supported.
Do you provide meals and snacks? ▼
Yes! All full-day programs include a healthy breakfast, a hot lunch, and two nutritious snacks. Our menus are created by a registered nutritionist, nut-free, and accommodate common allergies and dietary restrictions. Weekly menus are shared via the parent app.
How do you handle sick children? ▼
For the health of all children and staff, we ask that sick children stay home until they are fever-free for 24 hours without medication. If a child becomes ill during the day, we will contact parents immediately. Our sick child policy is shared in full during enrollment.
Is there a waitlist? How do I secure a spot? ▼
Availability varies by program and age group. We recommend contacting us as early as possible — especially for infant care, which fills quickly. A deposit is required to secure your spot on our enrollment roster.
Do you accept childcare subsidies or financial assistance? ▼
Yes, we accept state childcare assistance programs, Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF) subsidies, and employer-sponsored dependent care FSA benefits. We also offer a 10% sibling discount. Please speak with our enrollment coordinator for details.
Can I visit my child during the day? ▼
Absolutely. We have an open-door policy for enrolled families. We ask that you check in at the front desk upon arrival. Many parents enjoy joining us for special events, lunches, and classroom celebrations throughout the year.
